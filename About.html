Written on trains, in tea houses, and once on the back of a menu.

Compass & Ink started as a way to remember routes well enough to describe them to a friend. It's grown into a full journal of the trip — the good stretches, the wrong turns, and the costs nobody puts in the brochure.

Hi, I'm Emaan

I left a desk job with a single backpack and no real plan beyond "keep going until the money or the curiosity runs out." So far, it's been curiosity that's held up.

This journal is written in the evenings, usually from whatever guesthouse has the most reliable plug socket. I write about the places between the famous places — the towns you pass through on the way somewhere else, that turn out to be the whole point.
